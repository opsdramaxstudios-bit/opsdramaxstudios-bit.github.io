Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy describes how Automate Simple ("we") handles information in connection with our website and with Automate Simple Uploader (the "App"), an internal tool that uses the YouTube API Services.

Use of YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using the App you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What the App accesses

  • Permission to upload videos, thumbnails and video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, playlists) to the authorizing YouTube channel.
  • Read-only access to that channel's videos and YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources).

The App is used only by the Automate Simple team, with our own Google account, to manage our own YouTube channel. It does not access other users' accounts.

How information is used and stored

  • Upload permissions are used only to publish our own videos.
  • Analytics data is used only to prepare internal performance reports for our channel.
  • OAuth tokens and analytics reports are stored in private, access-controlled storage used by our team and are not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except service providers that process data on our behalf.
  • We do not use the App to collect personal information from YouTube viewers.

Retention and deletion

Analytics reports are kept for as long as needed for internal reporting and are deleted on request. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. When access is revoked, we delete stored tokens, and any stored YouTube API data is deleted within 30 days.

Website

This website does not use cookies or analytics trackers and does not collect personal information.
